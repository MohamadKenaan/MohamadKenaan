I'm a bioinformatician working at the intersection of **transcriptomics, computational genomics, epigenomics, and machine learning**. I enjoy turning complex sequencing datasets into reproducible analyses that help answer biological questions, particularly in neuroimmunology and human disease.

I currently work at **IRCCS Ospedale San Raffaele** in Milan, where I develop R and Python workflows for large-scale transcriptomic data analysis. I hold an MSc in **Molecular Biology and Genetics — Molecular Digital Biology** from the University of Pavia, with thesis research conducted at the University of Helsinki.

## 🔬 What I work on

- **Single-cell and single-nucleus transcriptomics:** quality control, cell-type analysis, differential expression, pseudobulk modeling, pathway analysis, and trajectory inference.
- **Machine learning for transcriptomics:** preprocessing, batch correction, feature selection, cross-validation, and disease classification using blood RNA-seq datasets.
- **Long-read genomics and epigenomics:** PacBio HiFi data processing, structural-variant discovery, genomic inversions, and DNA methylation analysis.
- **Reproducible scientific computing:** R, Python, Bash, Linux, and SLURM-based HPC workflows.

## 🧪 Selected research experience

### Neuroimmunology and transcriptomics | IRCCS Ospedale San Raffaele
*Bioinformatician · January 2026–present*

I analyze human and mouse transcriptomic datasets to investigate molecular and cellular processes in neurological disease. My work combines single-nucleus/single-cell RNA-seq, pseudobulk differential expression, gene-set enrichment, and machine-learning workflows for blood transcriptomics.

### Genomic inversions and DNA methylation | University of Helsinki
*MSc thesis researcher · February–October 2025*

I investigated a large genomic inversion in *Betula pendula* using PacBio HiFi sequencing, structural-variant analysis, and DNA methylation profiling. I built Bash/SLURM workflows for processing long-read sequencing data and characterizing variation across samples.
